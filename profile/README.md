## What we build

We build tools for controlling, measuring and documenting AI systems: explicit permissions, bounded execution, human approval and traceable results.

This organization is the new home for coordinated development. The published repositories linked below remain under `we-do-care-global` while their versions and integration boundaries are reviewed.

## Six published systems

| System | Responsibility | Published source | Explore |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AgentGuard** | Policy evaluation, rate limits and hash-linked audit records for agent tool calls. | [Repository](https://github.com/we-do-care-global/agentguard) | [Overview](https://wedocare-global.com/agentguard.html) |
| **FoundationCentar** | Agent orchestration, bounded missions and human governance. | [Repository](https://github.com/we-do-care-global/FoundationCentar) | [Overview](https://wedocare-global.com/foundationcentar.html) |
| **Enterprise Hybrid-RAG** | BM25 and vector retrieval combined through Reciprocal Rank Fusion. | [Repository](https://github.com/we-do-care-global/enterprise-hybrid-rag) | [Overview](https://wedocare-global.com/enterprise-hybrid-rag.html) |
| **Agent Eval** | Tool-call traces, latency, errors and agent metrics. | [Repository](https://github.com/we-do-care-global/agent-eval) | [Overview](https://wedocare-global.com/agent-eval.html) |
| **AgentVault** | Tool registration, secrets lifecycle, access policies and telemetry. | [Repository](https://github.com/we-do-care-global/agentvault) | [Overview](https://wedocare-global.com/agentvault.html) |
| **Pharma Intelligence OS** | Pharmacovigilance and regulatory intelligence workflows with human verification. | [Repository](https://github.com/we-do-care-global/pharma-intelligence-os) | [Overview](https://wedocare-global.com/pharma-intelligence-os.html) |

**Published** means source and publication records are available. Read each project's documented scope: demonstrations, development capabilities and verified integrations are distinguished from production claims. Pharma Intelligence OS is software, not a medical-care service.

## Engineering principles

- **Human oversight:** explicit decisions and approval boundaries.
- **Evidence:** traceable execution, sources and reproducible checks.
- **Control:** scoped tools, budgets and clear failure behavior.
- **Transparency:** documented limitations and inspectable source.

## Purpose

Our engineering work is grounded in a humanitarian purpose: technology should improve people's ability to act responsibly and support vulnerable communities. The founder's commitment is documented in the [humanitarian constitution](https://github.com/we-do-care-global/we-do-care-global/blob/main/CONSTITUTION_33PCT.md).
